90 Seconds to Nirvana Breathwork

Chapters
Breathe Along — Part 2

Practice 90 Seconds to Nirvana with me.

This session is made to follow along with while you do your own breathwork. The instruction is kept to a minimum for a more focused practice — come back to Part 1 anytime you need the instructions. Practice sitting or lying down somewhere safe, never while driving or in water.

Overview

A three-part breath you can come back to anytime you feel wound up.

The first and third breaths are the same: a deep inhale through the nose, a spear breath out through pursed lips, and a hold where you lengthen your spine, lightly tighten, and soften everything else. The second breath is a rhythm of up to 30 deep belly breaths, in and out. The first hold is 15 seconds; the last is 30 seconds — or longer, as your practice grows.

Start where you are. If 30 breaths is too many, do 10 or 15 and add to it later. If you have a heart condition or any health concern, keep it gentle and talk with a qualified healthcare professional first. Practice sitting or lying down somewhere safe — never while driving or in water.

Main Ideas

How the breath works.

0101

Three breaths, two the same

The first and third breaths match: in through the nose, a spear breath out, then a hold.

0202

Lengthen, tighten, soften

During the holds, lengthen your spine, lightly tighten from root to solar plexus, and soften everything else.

0303

Back and forth

The second breath alternates deep inhales and exhales, helping break you out of cycles you’re stuck in.

0404

Up to 30 breaths

If you can’t get that far, that’s okay. Do as many as you can, and add to it later.

0505

Any version helps

Three rounds is ideal, but a quick ten-breath round when you feel wound up is still great.

0606

Keep learning

Don’t be scared about the process. You’ll keep learning, and you’ll keep getting better.
