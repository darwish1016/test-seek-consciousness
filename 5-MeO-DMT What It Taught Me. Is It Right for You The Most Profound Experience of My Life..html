5-MeO-DMT: What It Taught Me. Is It Right for You? The Most Profound Experience of My Life.

This was one of the most profound experiences of my life. But what stayed with me was not the intensity. It was what happened when the usual sense of self disappeared for a moment — and what I had to learn afterward about surrender, connection, and practice.

5-MeO-DMT · ~6.5 min watch · Branches
Transcript Highlights Click any moment to jump

This is probably the most profound experience I have ever had, and even now it is difficult to explain because words only point toward something that had to be felt directly.

Before you experience something like this, it can sound like a story. Then something happens that changes the frame you were using to understand yourself.

For me, the turning point was surrender. The more completely control dropped away, the less the normal sense of 'me' seemed to be there.

What I experienced felt like pure white intelligent light, pure consciousness, something beyond the normal sense of separation.

There was no normal boundary between me and everything else. The experience felt completely non-dual — less like I was connected to something and more like there was only one thing happening.

The feeling was peace, bliss, and a sense of home that was difficult to compare with anything in normal waking life.

It felt like dropping beneath conditioning, protection, identity, and all the layers that had built up over time.

The experience felt like those layers were being stripped away until there was nothing left to hide behind.

That changed the direction of my path. Not because I suddenly had every answer, but because I could not relate to myself in exactly the same way afterward.

I describe 5-MeO-DMT as an entheogenic experience because, for me, it was less about belief and more about a direct encounter with what I understood as God, source, or consciousness.

What mattered most was what the experience showed me underneath the ego and the usual story I was carrying about myself.

I do not think this is for everyone. A powerful experience deserves respect, preparation, and a real understanding that you cannot control every part of what may come up.

The biggest mistake would be thinking the medicine itself is the entire path. I still had to build practices that helped me integrate what I experienced.

Breath, meditation, awareness, and stillness became ways of reconnecting with some of the same qualities without trying to recreate the original state.

The deeper lesson for me was connection — one consciousness, one field, love, and the feeling that separation was not as absolute as I had believed.

You do not need the medicine to keep returning to what the experience pointed toward. The doorway and the daily practice are not the same thing.

Eventually you realize you are the medicine. This was just the reminder.

After that, the path is how you live.

Chapters
Start With The Experience

Some things cannot be understood from the outside.

I can tell you what I felt. I can tell you that the normal sense of self disappeared, that separation seemed to fall away, and that what was left felt like pure consciousness, love, and something I can only describe as home.

But I also think this is where words reach their limit. My experience is not a definition of reality and it is not a promise of what someone else will experience. What mattered for me was that it changed how I saw the ego, identity, connection, and the work that still had to happen when I came back.

What It Taught Me

The experience showed me something. Practice taught me how to live with it.

0101

Surrender changed everything

The more control dropped away, the less the experience felt like something I was doing. For me, surrender was the doorway into everything that followed.

0202

The ego is not the whole of you

For a moment, the normal identity I carried was simply not there in the same way. That made me question how much of 'me' was conditioning, protection, habit, and story.

0303

Connection became direct

Instead of thinking about unity as an idea, I experienced something that felt completely without separation. I can only speak to what that felt like for me.

0404

Respect matters

Powerful altered states are not casual experiences and they are not for everyone. Curiosity is not the same thing as readiness, and another person's experience is not a reason to force one of your own.

0505

Integration became the real work

Coming back did not mean the work was finished. Breath, meditation, awareness, stillness, and honest changes in my life were what helped me begin integrating what the experience opened.

0606

You do not need to keep chasing the doorway

Eventually the deeper question became whether I could reconnect with presence, love, and awareness through the way I lived — not whether I could reproduce one extraordinary experience.

Sit With This

What Would Still Matter Without the Experience?

You do not have to share Kurt's interpretation to use the questions. Bring them back to your own direct experience.

  1. 01

    What parts of your identity feel deeply true, and what parts may simply be roles, habits, protection, or stories you have carried for a long time?

  2. 02

    Where in your life do you notice the strongest need to control what happens next?

  3. 03

    Have you ever had a moment — through nature, meditation, love, grief, music, or anything else — where the usual sense of separation softened?

  4. 04

    If a profound experience showed you something important, what would integration look like once the intensity was gone?

  5. 05

    What practice could help you return to presence or connection without needing to chase a particular state?

Try This: Sit quietly for a few minutes and forget about trying to create an extraordinary state. Notice what is here when you stop adding more. Your breath. Your body. The room. The fact that you are aware. Start there.

Where This Fits In The Blueprint

Branches → Psychedelics, Catalysts & Expanded States

This branch explores psychedelics, entheogens, and other catalysts that can open deeper states of consciousness, reveal hidden patterns, and reconnect you with parts of yourself that are harder to access in everyday life. But the experience is only the beginning. What matters is what you do with what is revealed.

See It In The Blueprint
  1. Different medicines, different ways in — each catalyst has its own effects, its own intelligence, and its own way of working with you.
  2. See what is normally hidden — these experiences can bring patterns, emotions, and parts of yourself to the surface that may have been sitting underneath everything else.
  3. Reconnect with something deeper — some expanded states can bring you closer to what feels underneath the ego, conditioning, and the identity you have built.
  4. The experience can open the door — but you are still the one who has to walk through it. What is revealed only matters if you work with it afterward.
  5. Integration is what makes the difference — bring what you saw back into your actual life. These tools can support your path, but they do not define it.

These are powerful tools, and they deserve respect. They are not for everyone. Take your time. Learn. Feel into it. Start slowly. And decide what actually feels right for you.

This page reflects Kurt's personal experience. Powerful altered states can carry psychological and physical risks, and another person's experience should not be treated as a guarantee of what yours would be.
