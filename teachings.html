The Five Mind Tools

These tools are deeply connected. One helps you notice what is taking your attention. Another helps you stop feeding it. Another brings you back to one point. Flow is what you may begin to experience when less and less is getting in the way.

  1. 01
    Mind Tool 01

    The Cat and the Mouse Method

    Notice each thought as it appears. If it doesn’t need you right now, let it pass and come back to what is actually here.

    Open Teaching →~3 min
  2. 02
    Mind Tool 02

    The Replacement Loop

    When a loop starts pulling you away, don’t fight it. Replace it with something real you can actually feel.

    Open Teaching →~3 min
  3. 03
    Mind Tool 03

    Single Point Focus

    Bring all of your attention to the one thing in front of you, and notice how different it feels.

    Open Teaching →~3 min
