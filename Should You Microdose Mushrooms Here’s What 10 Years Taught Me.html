After more than 10 years of working with mushroom microdoses, the biggest thing I notice is not some dramatic change. It is the smaller shifts — a little more presence, a little more openness, a little more connection to what is already here.

Start With The Subtlety

The value is not always in how much you feel. Sometimes it is in what you begin to notice.

When I first started working with mushroom microdoses, it was not because I had some huge philosophy around them. It was practical. Life gets busy. A lighter experience gave me another way to explore without stepping completely out of the rest of my day.

What 10 Years Taught Me

Small shifts can still change the way you experience your life.

0101

It does not have to be dramatic

Microdosing is not about chasing a full psychedelic experience. The shift can sit quietly underneath what you are already doing.

0202

Presence can feel different

One of the biggest things I notice is a little more openness and connection. Music, conversation, nature, and even your own space can feel different when you are actually there for them.

0303

Connection is what stands out

For me, mushrooms tend to have a strong feeling of connection — to yourself, another person, nature, creativity, music, and the present moment.

0404

The same patterns can loosen

Sometimes the value is simply stepping far enough outside the usual loop to see it from another angle. You may notice something that was already there but harder to see before.

0505

Subtlety is part of the point

This is not about intensity. Small shifts can be enough. A little more awareness in the way you listen, create, talk, or move through the day can begin showing up elsewhere in your life.

0606

You still choose what belongs in your path

Curiosity does not mean obligation. Take your time. Do your own research. Notice what actually feels right for you. And if it is not for you, that is completely fine too.

You do not need a dramatic experience to learn something about yourself. Notice what becomes clearer when you pay closer attention.

    What parts of your normal day do you tend to move through without really noticing them?

  2. 02

    When do you naturally feel most present — music, conversation, nature, creativity, movement, stillness, or something else?

  3. 03

    What is one familiar pattern you would like to see from a different angle rather than immediately trying to change it?

  4. 04

    What does connection actually feel like for you when you are not trying to force it?

  5. 05

    Are you usually drawn to intensity, or can you recognize value in smaller shifts that unfold more slowly?

  6. 06

    If you are curious about any catalyst, what would taking your time, doing your own research, and making a genuinely intentional choice look like for you?
