How I Found What I’d Been Searching for My Entire Life—and What It Could Mean for You

For a long time, I was looking for something outside of myself. What I eventually discovered was that it had been within me the entire time. I simply didn’t know how to access it.

Full Transcript Click any line to jump

Today I want to share what I’ve been searching for my entire life. How I found it and what this could mean for you.

From a very young age I was always drawn to the esoteric. I always wanted to know more. I was fascinated by things like cryptozoology, ancient yogis, mystics, whatever I could get my hands on. people who had access to deeper states of consciousness, even things that felt beyond normal human experience. It completely fascinated me. I had to know more. So, I kept digging deeper and deeper. I read everything I could, traveled the world, studied with masters. Back then, you couldn’t just hop on the internet and ask AI. You had to go search for it yourself by hand, flipping through card catalogs, digging through the library. You really had to want it. And it makes me appreciate that we have everything at our fingertips now. I always knew there was something more, something deeper, an underlying truth that I wasn’t connected to, something I knew was there, but I couldn’t quite reach. And even though I was content, I knew there was something missing.

I’ve always been drawn to the biggest questions. What are we actually doing here? What is God? Not as an idea, but as something you can experience, what are the secrets of death that you can understand while you’re still alive? I could sense there was more. I just didn’t fully understand it yet. And for a long time, I was looking for something outside of myself. But all this time, it was already within me.

What I eventually realized is that none of this information exists outside of us. Everything we’re looking for is already within us. The problem was I just didn’t know how to access it. Once you truly realize that, you don’t just understand it intellectually, you feel it from within. I started to notice I was chasing the wrong things, not able to see what really actually mattered. It’s so easy to get pulled into thinking material things are the most important. But at the end of the day, you can’t take any of that with you. The only things that carry forward are your spirit, your love, and your growth. I spent a lot of my time distracting myself, keeping busy, filling space. So, I didn’t actually have to look within.

I was caught in mental loops, going through the motions, and not really living my truth. At some point though, you run out of ways to distract yourself and you’re left with the truth. There was a point in my life where I realized I was living a lie. I wasn’t embodying my full potential. I was scared to change. The life I had built was so comfortable. Even though I knew I wasn’t living my truth, I’ve always been willing to go deeper, to look into the darkness because I knew that’s where the real answers lied.

I had to completely reimagine myself, shift what I was doing, and start creating an entirely different future. Making that leap was the most important thing I’ve ever done. And I’ll say this because I know a lot of people feel it.

The things that make you uncomfortable are often pointing in the exact direction you need to go. We’re all a little afraid. That’s normal. But if you want more, you have to be willing to take the chance to put it out there and actually go for it.

The realization didn’t fully land for me until I sat in a 5-MeO-DMT ceremony. Within about 30 seconds, there was no self. There’s no Kurt. There’s no me. I simply merged into the entire universe. Everything and nothing all at the same time. Infinitely big and infinitely small. This isn’t an ego death. It’s a complete ego dissolution. Not something I understood with my mind. Something I felt through the intelligence of my entire body. In that state, you experience a direct knowing, feeling the oneness of everything, the beauty of God or source, which has often been referred to throughout mankind’s history as nonduality. And many traditions across the world have been pointing to this truth, this eternal truth for thousands of years.

When I came back to the material realm, everything was different. Not just the experience, my entire life. What mattered, what didn’t, who I spent time with, what I put into my body physically, mentally, and spiritually. How I saw myself, not just as a body, but as something much deeper. That one shift changed everything. And I don’t mean that lightly. It changed how I live, how I think, how I relate to people, how I move through life, how I’m sitting here right now talking to you.

I’ve been good at a lot of things, a multifaceted artist. I’ve built multiple businesses, a loving father, a devoted friend, but this is different. This is the one place where everything clicks, where nothing feels forced. It just feels effortless, natural, like I’m finally aligned with what I’m actually meant to be doing. And what I found is that as I went deeper into this, it didn’t pull me away from life. Oddly enough, it made me better at everything I did. More present, more focused, more intentional. Because when you’re aligned, you bring your full attention into what you’re doing. Most people think of enlightenment like a switch. You either have it or you don’t.

But it’s not like that. It’s more like an infinite dial that you can keep spinning deeper and deeper and deeper for as long as you want. And at its core, this path isn’t complicated. It’s about becoming more present and more loving. But to actually live that, you need the right framework, the right tools, and real integration.

I could never go back to living the old way. There’s no amount of money. There’s no amount of anything that would make me trade in what I understand and feel now. Because once you see and feel your truth, you can’t unsee it. And when you’re not living it, you start dying on the inside. slowly, quietly, until something finally breaks.

The reason I’m doing this is because I feel so good, and I want other people to have access to this in whatever way works for them. We all know it doesn’t work when someone tells you what to do. I’m simply here as a humble guide so you can find your own version of what feels right and what resonates with you. You can go as deep as you want or just take a few tools and feel more grounded, more clear, more connected. The more you tune in, the closer you come to your true self and your true path. And the closer you are to that, the closer you are to love, clarity, and fully embodying who you’re here to be. Because this is your path. I’m just here to help you see it a little more clearly.
