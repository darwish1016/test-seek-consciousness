Chapters
Overview

Manifestation is not only the destination. The entire journey is part of it.

Manifestation is much more than thinking positively about something you want and hoping the universe brings it to you. It begins with understanding what you’re creating, where that desire comes from, what you believe is possible for you, and who you’re becoming as you move toward it.

Hold the vision deeply — see it, feel it, believe it — and take the steps you can see. Then stay open to the pieces you can’t see yet. And don’t become so focused on creating your future that you stop experiencing the life already happening around you.
